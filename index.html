About Global Leaf Plantation

Global Leaf Plantation (Pvt) Ltd was established in July 2025 as a limited company under the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007. We are committed to developing sustainable plantation solutions, supporting local agriculture, and creating valuable growth opportunities for our investors and communities.

With modern agricultural practices and a dedicated professional team, we aim to build a greener future while contributing to Sri Lanka's economic growth.
